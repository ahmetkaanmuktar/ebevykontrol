Privacy Policy — Parental Control

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Offline Architecture

Our app operates on a local-first, offline-by-design architecture:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does NOT request or use android.permission.INTERNET.
  • No External Servers: We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: Your data never leaves your device. No analytics SDKs or ad networks.

2. Permissions & How We Use Them

A) Android AccessibilityService API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Purpose: To detect when a daily time-restricted application is opened and present the parental lock screen when allowed time has expired.

What is Monitored: Only the package name of the active foreground application.

What is NEVER Monitored: Keystrokes, passwords, private chats, screen contents (canRetrieveWindowContent="false"), camera, or photos are STRICTLY NOT MONITORED.

B) Package Usage Stats (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Purpose: To calculate daily screen time usage (in minutes) for installed applications to enforce daily quotas set by parents.

C) Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Purpose: To display the lock screen when daily time limits are reached.

D) Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC)

Purpose: To allow parents to quickly authenticate with fingerprint/face unlock for configuration or granting temporary extra time (+15 mins).

3. Data Security & Storage

PINs are hashed using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 with 10,000 iterations and a unique 16-byte cryptographic salt. Databases are strictly excluded from Android cloud backups.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Designed solely as a tool for parents and legal guardians. No child personal information is ever collected or processed.
